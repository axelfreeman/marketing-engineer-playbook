# 01 — The method

A marketing engineer is the person who makes marketing measurable and portable. Four primitives, in the order
the numbers demand them:

1. **Offer.** One sentence that names the customer and the change. No channel rescues a sentence nobody wants.
2. **Page.** The offer lives at a URL that loads in under a second, states the price, and answers the five
   questions a buyer actually asks.
3. **Source.** Query-intent pages written to be quoted by answer engines: definitions, comparisons, "how to
   choose", written completely, linked to each other.
4. **Measurement.** One event for the outcome that matters — usually a click into a conversation — fired into
   the client's own analytics property.

Everything else (ads, sequences, tooling) is a multiplier on those four. Multipliers on zero are zero.

## The order of operations

- Positioning before copy. Copy before the page. Page before the channel. Channel before scale.
- If a proposal line cannot be reduced to a URL or a login the client controls, it is not an asset.
- Every claim ships with a link that opens.
